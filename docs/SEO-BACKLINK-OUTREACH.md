# PaperSource Ghana backlink outreach

Backlinks should be earned from pages that genuinely help the referring audience. Do not buy links, exchange links at scale, submit to automated directories, or ask partners to use keyword-stuffed anchor text.

## Priority opportunities

1. **Group and sister companies**
   Ask NiiPlants Group, NiiPlants Logistics, NiiPlants Ghana, and Trivoxo to list PaperSource as the group's stationery/workplace-supplies business. Use the canonical homepage URL: `https://www.papersourcegh.com/`.

2. **Existing clients and procurement partners**
   Ask confirmed clients and suppliers whether they maintain an approved-vendor, procurement, or supplier-partner page. Request a factual listing only where PaperSource is genuinely a supplier or partner.

3. **Ghanaian schools and organisations**
   Offer a useful stationery checklist or procurement guide that schools and organisations can reference. The link should point to the specific guide, not always the homepage.

4. **Local business profiles**
   Complete verified Google Business Profile and legitimate Ghana business-directory listings with identical name, phone, location, hours, and website details. Avoid duplicate or low-quality directory submissions.

## Linkable resources to publish

- Ghana office stationery procurement checklist
- A4 paper sizes and GSM explained
- School stationery list for Ghanaian classrooms
- Accra, Tema, nationwide delivery and shop-pickup guide
- Printer toner compatibility checklist

Each resource should contain original PaperSource guidance, a clear author/business attribution, relevant product/category links, and a last-reviewed date. Do not publish thin pages solely to attract links.

## Measurement

- Record the referring domain, contact, target resource, date contacted, and response.
- Monitor referring domains and landing-page engagement in Search Console and Analytics.
- Review links quarterly and request removal of inaccurate, outdated, or unauthorised listings.
